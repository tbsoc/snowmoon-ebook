About the novel

Snowmoon follows two lives in a far-future world of rival nations. In Veridia, a lush, privacy-minded democracy, Gladias is training with the Order of Steering, the semi-anonymous body whose taxes and subsidies gently nudge how everyone lives, until a stranger begins urging him to turn the system against itself. In Dzego, a nation that lost a war a century ago and is still harassed by the Arctic Empire, coordination happens less through the government than through anonymous cryptographic networks and secret societies. There, Zei, a teenage prodigy at a physics-based strategy game, is caught up in an attack on his school.

As pressure from the Arctic Empire grows, the novel asks what lets a society be both free and strong. It is an idea-driven political science fiction story, full of experiments in governance such as quadratic voting and public-goods funding, and it explores privacy as civic infrastructure, open versus closed technology, and the cost of failing to cooperate.

About the author

Vitalik Buterin is a programmer and writer best known as the co-founder of Ethereum. Born in Russia in 1994 and raised in Canada, he co-founded Bitcoin Magazine in 2011, proposed Ethereum in a 2013 white paper, and saw it launch in 2015.

He writes long-form essays on his blog about cryptography, governance, mechanism design and public-goods funding, and co-authored the paper that introduced quadratic funding. A collection of his essays, Proof of Stake, was published in 2022. Snowmoon, released free on his website in 2026, is his first full-length novel.
